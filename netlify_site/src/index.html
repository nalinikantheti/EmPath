Why it matters

A daily destination can be a connection to everyday life.

A temple can mean community. A grocery store can mean independence. A park can mean a reason to leave the house. When walking is difficult, being close on a map is not enough.

For households, the question is “Could we reach what we need?” For planners, it is “Where could a better connection, crossing or shade make a difference?” EmPath makes those questions visible, so people can investigate the routes behind them.

More than distance. Still an estimate.

A short walk can feel longer beside traffic, with limited tree cover or on hot surfaces. EmPath combines distance with estimated tree, heat and traffic context to compare walking effort.

It does not certify safety or personal accessibility. Routes start from area reference points; sidewalks, crossings, entrances and opening hours need checking.
