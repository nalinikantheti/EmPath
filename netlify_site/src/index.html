Nearby should

Imagine living in Plano without a car. Your temple is close—but the walk means exposed pavement, little shade and a busy crossing. On a hot day, a short trip can become a serious barrier.

Getting to worship, groceries or a pharmacy should not depend on having a car. EmPath helps you look beyond distance: compare areas, then see the actual walk.

More than distance. Still an estimate.

A short walk can feel longer beside traffic, with limited tree cover or on hot surfaces. EmPath combines distance with estimated tree, heat and traffic context to compare walking effort.

It does not certify safety or personal accessibility. Routes start from area reference points; sidewalks, crossings, entrances and opening hours need checking.
