How to interpret these maps. The combined index uses assumed equal weights for canopy deficit and nearby traffic. It is not a validated measure of personal comfort or danger. The GeoAI demonstration covers small imagery areas; full-area inference is a separate work in progress. Routes, crossings and entrances are unverified. Regional summer weather is shared context, not a block-level temperature measurement.
