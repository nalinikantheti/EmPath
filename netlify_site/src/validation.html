2. Calculation and interface checks

  • Finite, nonnegative edge weights and the documented cost formula.
  • Reverse multi-source category costs match forward Dijkstra spot checks.
  • Each displayed route matches its cached category cost; a complete selection averages to the same block score.
  • Category selection, empty selections, ranking filters, route highlighting, category changes and CSV download tested in Chrome.
  • City boundary and three-kilometer routing/destination buffer are explicit.

These checks establish implementation consistency. They do not certify real-world sidewalk access or safety.

Correcting search-extent effects for public short walks

The production network now includes a 3 km ring beyond Plano, 290,552 finite weighted edges, expanded destinations and matching GeoAI tree, traffic and Landsat context.

Public budgets are 10, 20 or 30 effort minutes per destination. The minimum conservative outer-boundary lower bound among housing-positive, ≤100 m-connection origins is 40.6 minutes. Every public recommendation must pass its own origin-specific bound.

Because each edge costs at least its walking time, a trip outside the search region must first reach the outer boundary. If that already costs more than the chosen budget, no outside candidate or detour can improve a qualifying trip or become reachable within that budget under the fixed model. Distance bounds use a 1% projection safety allowance, checked against source geometry lengths.

This corrects the acquisition-extent effect for bounded public comparisons. It does not certify that all real paths, services or entrances are mapped. Proxy uncertainty, excluded connectors and crossing waits remain. Longer unrestricted advanced-map trips above the origin-specific bound remain qualified.

Compared with the prior 1 km weighted run, 6,523 block/category pairs decrease by more than one effort minute. Some increase because environmental aggregation and catalog/snap extraction also changed; this is not a controlled boundary-only treatment effect.
